Research focus

Compliance as a
design tool.

My research combines mechanism design, handed shearing auxetics (HSA), underactuation, passive differentials, finite-element analysis, fabrication, and experimental validation.

I work on two connected directions: adaptive end-effectors for delicate object handling, and multi-DoF compliant wrist modules for prosthetic applications. Before my PhD, I worked on rehabilitation-device development at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Joint supervision: Prof. Giovanni Berselli, University of Genoa, and Prof. Cristina Piazza, TUM/MIRMI.
